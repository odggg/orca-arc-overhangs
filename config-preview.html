Arc Overhang

Concentric arc toolpaths for flat overhangs. Applied during G-code export.

Arc spacing and wall anchoring are independent. Spacing is a factor of nozzle diameter: 0.5 with a 0.4 mm nozzle gives 0.2 mm spacing.
